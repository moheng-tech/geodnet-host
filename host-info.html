What Is the Program?

📡A single compact device — that's it

The station is an all-in-one unit that combines the antenna and GPS receiver in a single weatherproof enclosure. It mounts outside (typically on a roof or pole), plugs into your internet connection, and GEODNET handles everything else remotely. You won't need to interact with it on a day-to-day basis.

🌐Part of a growing precision GPS network

GEODNET operates a global network of high-accuracy GPS reference stations. Your location helps improve positioning accuracy for commercial and industrial applications in your area — including surveying, autonomous vehicles, and precision agriculture.

Frequently Asked Questions

🔧Who installs and maintains the equipment?

The physical installation will be done by you, the participant. GEODNET provides all equipment and manages the system remotely. You don't need any technical expertise to be a host.

💸Is there any cost to me as a host?

GEODNET supplies the hardware and manages the station remotely at no cost to you. The host is responsible for the physical installation — GEODNET does not cover installation labor. The only things you provide are a mounting location, the installation itself, and an internet connection.

⚡How much power does it use?

The unit runs on 12V and uses less than 5W — less than a phone charger. It is designed to run continuously at minimal energy cost.

📶Does it affect my internet speed?

The station uses approximately 10 GB per month to transmit GPS correction data. This is a modest amount — roughly equivalent to streaming a few hours of video — and is unlikely to noticeably impact your normal internet use on most home plans.

🏠Will it damage my roof or property?

The unit is compact and lightweight. It mounts using non-invasive hardware designed to avoid property damage. If you have specific concerns about your property, we're happy to discuss them before you commit to installation.

🚚What happens if the program ends or I want to stop?

If you decide to leave the program or GEODNET discontinues service, we simply arrange to have the equipment shipped back to us. There is no penalty for ending participation.

📋Is there a contract?

Yes — a simple host agreement is provided before installation. It outlines the payment terms, equipment ownership (GEODNET's), and the process for ending the arrangement.
